Owner-first enrichment · Claude vision
Document extractor — pull the owner the portal hides.
When a permit's front page shows no owner and no contractor, the real contacts live in the attached PDFs — the signed application, SEPA checklist, and financial-responsibility form. Seattle publishes these in its Electronic Document Library (EDMS) at web6.seattle.gov/dpd/edms, searchable by case number or address since 2006. Drop the PDF here and Claude reads it with vision to extract the owner, phone, email, architect, and whether a GC is already named — the "no-GC" signal that says you're early.
